Imava is a modern, privacy-focused media gallery designed for security and speed. It operates fully offline with no tracking, no advertisements, and no analytics.

Key features include:
* <b>Cryptographic Secure File Shredder:</b> Overwrites deleted files with multi-pass CSPRNG random bytes, zeroes out file headers, and flushes hardware write caches to prevent recovery by forensic tools.
* <b>Hidden Vault:</b> Separate encrypted container protected by biometric authentication or custom PIN, with support for decoy passwords and stealth mode.
* <b>Guest Hand-Off Mode:</b> Locks the photo viewer to a single image and disables swipe navigation, preventing guests from browsing through your other media until unlocked.
* <b>Ghost Thumbnail Purger:</b> Scans and securely shreds unencrypted thumbnail cache files lingering in DCIM and cache directories.
* <b>Privacy Sharing & EXIF Fuzzing:</b> Strips metadata or offsets GPS coordinates by ~2 km before sharing to preserve regional context without revealing exact locations.
* <b>Deep Media Scanner:</b> Instant detection of media stored in hidden folders, .nomedia directories, WhatsApp Sent/Private, and Telegram directories.
* <b>Media Management:</b> High-performance timeline with pinch-to-zoom grid scaling, album pinning, duplicate cleaning, and built-in video player.
