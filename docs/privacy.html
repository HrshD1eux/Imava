Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026 · Last Updated: October 29, 2026

1. Fundamental Philosophy: Privacy by Design

Imava ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed and maintained by Harsh Pahal (@HrshD1eux), is built on the unwavering premise that your personal photos, videos, and memories belong exclusively to you.

The Core Promise: Imava does not collect, harvest, monetize, transmit, or share any personal data, media files, facial recognition models, EXIF metadata, or device telemetry with any third party, remote server, or analytics service. Everything stays strictly on your physical device.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Unlike conventional cloud-tethered galleries, Imava operates under a strict zero-knowledge, zero-telemetry architecture:

  • No User Accounts: You do not register, create an account, or log in with an email address, phone number, or social media profile.
  • No Cloud Sync: Imava does not possess or operate any proprietary remote cloud storage servers. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to any remote computer.
  • No Telemetry or Tracking: Imava contains zero Google Analytics, zero Firebase, zero Facebook SDK, zero AdMob, and zero third-party crash reporting SDKs.
  • No Behavioral Profiling: We do not track which albums you open, how frequently you view photos, what tags you create, or your geographic location.

3. Permissions Requested & Technical Justification

Imava only requests Android runtime permissions that are technically required to display, organize, edit, and secure your media files locally. In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, here is an exhaustive description of every permission:

Permission Android API Specific Purpose & Scope
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Android 13+ (API 33+) Reads local image files on device to index thumbnails and display them in your timeline.
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO Android 13+ (API 33+) Reads local video files to generate duration overlays and enable local playback.
READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED Android 14+ (API 34+) Supports partial and selective photo access chosen by the user in the Android Photo Picker.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Android 8.0–12 (API 26–32) Provides backward compatibility to index photos and videos on legacy Android releases.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Android 8.0–10 (API 26–29) Enables moving, copying, renaming, and saving edited photos on legacy Android releases.
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Android 11+ (API 30+) Enables Deep Media Discovery, scanning WhatsApp sent media, custom hidden folders, and .nomedia directories.
ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION Android 10+ (API 29+) Extracts GPS coordinates from image EXIF headers solely for on-device location sorting and privacy fuzzing.
USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT Android 9.0+ (API 28+) Authenticates your fingerprint or device credentials locally to unlock the Private Vault. No biometric data ever leaves the hardware TEE.
SET_WALLPAPER / SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS All Versions Allows users to set any photo directly as home screen or lock screen wallpaper.
VIBRATE All Versions Powers tactile haptic vibrations during photo selection, tag manipulation, and vault unlocking.
INTERNET All Versions Declared strictly for optional user-initiated GitHub release checks and bug report submissions. 0 KB of photo data or analytics is ever sent over the internet.

4. Private Vault & Zero-Knowledge Cryptography

Imava features a hardware-backed Private Vault utilizing the Android KeyStore with authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption.

  • Keys are generated and stored within your device's Hardware Security Module (HSM), Trusted Execution Environment (TEE), or StrongBox Keymaster.
  • Master Passwords are never stored in plaintext. They are derived using 250,000 PBKDF2 rounds with SHA-256 and salted randomly.
  • Zero-Knowledge: We have zero technical ability to recover, reset, or bypass your password or PIN. If you lose your master password without a backup, your encrypted vault cannot be decrypted by anyone, including the developer.
  • Decoy PIN: Entering an alternate Decoy PIN opens an isolated, fake empty vault container for plausible deniability under coercion.

5. CSPRNG Forensic File Shredder

When you permanently delete a photo using Imava's Cryptographic Shredder, raw disk sectors are overwritten with multi-pass cryptographically secure pseudorandom bytes (CSPRNG) before the file inode is unlinked. This guarantees that file data cannot be recovered via forensic chip carving or NAND recovery software.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because Imava is an offline media viewer and does not collect any personally identifiable information, it complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Imava is completely safe for users of all ages.

8. Data Retention, Deletion & Your Rights

You retain absolute sovereignty over your data:

  • Because all data is stored exclusively on your device, uninstalling Imava or clearing its app data from Android System Settings instantly purges all local app databases, cache, and custom tag indices.
  • For our dedicated Data Deletion Policy statement, visit our Data Deletion Policy Page.
